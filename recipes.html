Crafting Codex

Pick something craftable and see every material underneath it — each intermediate craft, all the way down to the raw mats you actually have to gather or buy — with the morale and time it costs.

It only knows what people put in it. Neverwinter has hundreds of recipes and this site does not publish guessed numbers, so the Codex is being filled in by hand, by players with the game open. If you craft, the Add a Recipe tab is where you can help.

Copy what your workshop screen says, exactly as it spells it — the Codex matches materials to recipes by name, so "Adamant Ore" and "Adamantine Ore" are two different things to it. Only the item name, profession, tier and the material list are required; leave anything you cannot see blank rather than guessing at it. Submissions are checked before they appear.

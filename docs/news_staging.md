## Week of October 12, 2026

### Features

- **Dungeon Achievement Tracker** (new, under Creators & Tools). Track dungeon achievements, tick them off, and see which ones you are closest to finishing. It is a growing list (about 160 so far) with more being added. Search by dungeon, name or text; filter by dungeon, Not done / Done / All, and Master only; sort by closest to completion, dungeon, points or name. Tick the sub-achievements, keep counters like 9/25, and watch the progress bar fill. Built for phones first: one column, big buttons, and the filters tuck into a Filter sheet. Sign in with Discord (free, the same sign-in as Toon Forge cloud saves) and your progress follows you to any device, so clearing your browser no longer loses it. Signed out it saves in your browser only, and there is a Back up to a file / Restore from a file pair as a safety net. Entries whose details are not added yet are hidden by default (a toggle shows them).

- **Dungeon Achievement Tracker now keeps progress per character.** Dungeon achievements belong to a character in game, so the tracker now does too. Pick your character at the top of the page, add up to 10 ("+ Add character"), rename them, remove ones you no longer play, or copy progress from one character into another (it only adds, it never overwrites newer ticks). Search, filters, the closest-to-completion sort and the points-left count all follow the character you have selected, and the page remembers which one you were on. Everything you had already ticked is kept: it moves into a character called Main on every device, in the browser and in your Discord account. Back up to a file now saves all your characters, and older backup files still restore (into the character you are on).

### Bug Fixes
- **Tank builds: the optimizer now leaves your powers, class features and feats alone** (live 2026-10-18). Tanks pick those for holding aggro, which the optimizer can't measure, so for a tank it builds your gear and keeps your kit exactly as you set it. Also fixed: Faerie Fire (Drow racial) lasts 5 seconds, not 3.
- **Rogue Assassin tested in game** (live 2026-10-17). Stealth now refills in the fight (about 12.5 seconds from empty, usable again only when full, and ending it with an encounter empties it), and the simulator saves it for your best Stealth hit. Duelist's Flurry's Bleed now counts (about as much damage as the flurry itself at full stacks), and so does Shadow's Flurry. Oppressive Darkness, Assassinate and Toxic Blades confirmed as modelled. Also found: Recharge Speed on shirts and pants counts in real cooldowns, even though power tooltips leave it out.
- **Arbiter: more in-game tests, and the optimizer now keeps your divinity powers** (live 2026-10-16). Lightspeed makes your next Daunting Light instant (one cast per Searing Javelin); Divine Glow more than doubles divinity regen for 12 s; Critical Insight pays 10 divinity per crit on every target; Divine Equilibrium's curve and Channel Divinity's fixed refill speed confirmed. Critical Sun now works in the fight simulator: a Celestial Prominence crit gives a free recast within 6 seconds (before, the simulator never used it). Also from the tests: Flame Strike's burn now ticks 4 times (it was counted once), Piercing Javelin's guaranteed Searing Javelin crit now counts (it was worth nothing), Burning Patch and Tipping Scales confirmed as written. The fight simulator was dropping divinity-costing encounters too early on some builds; it now tries putting them back once it has worked out channelling and quick taps, which raises Arbiter results by about 10%.
- **Arbiter tested in game: Angel of Death and Perfect Balance** (live 2026-10-16). Recorded tests confirmed how Angel of Death builds and resets, and showed Perfect Balance works the opposite way round from its tooltip (Forgemaster's Flame builds Burning Shift). The fight simulator now clears both Shifts when one runs two ahead, refills divinity when both reach 4, and keeps the two even when that pays off. Channel Divinity's gauge refund now matches the measured numbers.
- **Toon Forge v2.2: Arbiter fixes from a full class audit** (live 2026-10-15). Angel of Death, Light of the Scales and Inner Balance now count in the fight; the simulator works out for itself how long to hold Channel Divinity and when to use Angel of Death; Doomsayer now counts Geas; and the optimizer now weighs divinity-max and divinity-regen gear properly.
- **Toon Forge v2.1: Arbiter clerics now run out of divinity in the fight simulator** (live 2026-10-15). Searing Javelin and Forgemaster's Flame cost divinity, but the simulator let them fire every second forever, so the optimizer overrated Arbiter rotations built on them. It now tracks your divinity bar (1,000, plus 150 with Expanded Faith), refills it naturally and by holding Channel Divinity (with the Judgement gauge refund), and stops attacking while you channel. Divinity-regeneration items now count for an Arbiter, and Divine Equilibrium reads your real divinity level. Thanks to the player whose recordings measured all of it.

# Threat mechanics — source of truth for the tank threat chart

Every number carries its source. Do not tighten an estimate without a measurement.

## Confirmed (developer statements, official patch notes, tooltips)

- Each enemy keeps its own threat list. Hate = D + H + T. D: 1 point of damage = 1 threat on that
  target. H: 1 point of healing = 1 threat, split evenly across every enemy the healer is in combat
  with. T: hidden flat threat added by powers carrying the "Increased Threat" tag (behaves like
  damage, invisible).
- Whoever holds aggro gets a 10% buffer the next person must exceed. Hard taunts set you to the top
  of the list and grant that buffer.
- Mod 16 preview (2019): tank role +200% (3x); Increased Threat powers about +700% (8x).
- Developer reply (Oct 2021): the tank role multiplier is 4.5x.
- Nov 7 2023 "Tank Threat Baseline Rework" (patch notes): tank powers WITHOUT Increased Threat
  roughly tripled; powers WITH Increased Threat about the same as before; class powers/feats that
  boost overall threat +33% each with diminishing returns per unique application; items that boost
  threat +6.6% each with diminishing returns; at-will threat buffs short, encounter threat buffs
  long; dailies and healing more valuable for threat; Divine Word, Dashing Decoy (Diamond set),
  Teamwork Tactics (Northern Jewel set) refactored to the scheme.
- Justicar (Nov 2023): Aura of Valor allies' threat -25%, a further -15% while Divine Champion is
  active, no longer affects other tanks. Intimidating Presence: ongoing high-threat damage to nearby
  enemies while Divine Champion is active, and increases threat generated.
- Vanguard (Nov 2023): Staying Power threat bonus applies to all powers, duration 5 s -> 10 s.
- Sentinel (Nov 2023): Boasting Takedown feat increases Takedown's threat. Rage and Rally (Mod 19):
  Unstoppable increases threat generated by attacks.
- Everfrost Icy Aggression 15% -> 5% max HP (Apr 2024). Jewel of the North: threat bonus to tanks
  (Mar 2022). Soulweaver healing threat reduced (Sep 2023); Soul Manipulation reduces warlock heal
  threat (Mod 19). Minsc no longer generates extra threat (Jun 2024).
- Justicar tooltips (verbatim, re-read 2026-09-11, all 16 attack screenshots): "Added Effect:
  Increased Threat" is on Oath Strike, Templar's Wrath and Vow of Enmity, and on Sacred Weapon
  (Justicar) ONLY while the Sacred Shield choice feat is slotted (n00b 2026-09-11; the May capture
  had it slotted; base tooltip without the feat not captured yet).
  NOT tagged: Shielding Strike (Stamina Restoration), Valorous Strike, Radiant Slam, Relentless
  Avenger, Burning Light (Stun), Smite, Bane, Divine Judgement, Radiant Charge (Knock Down), Binding
  Oath (Stamina Restoration), Divine Touch (heal). Vow of Enmity: "If you are not the affected
  targets' highest threat, place yourself at the top of their threat list."; Oath of Protection:
  "Threat generation greatly increased."

## Estimated (derived; always shown with an estimate marker)

- Untagged tank power multiplier: 4.5 x ~3 = about 13.5x damage-to-threat.
- Increased Threat power multiplier, PRIMARY reading (n00b 2026-09-11, the literal one): the three
  statements are three LAYERS. Role (Oath of Protection) 4.5x; untagged power x3 after the rework
  = 13.5x; tagged power x8, unchanged = 4.5 x 8 = 36x. The tag is worth 8/3 = about 2.7x an
  untagged hit. Chart uses this.
- Alternative reading (kept for the record): the 2019 "8x" was the whole multiplier, so tagged
  powers sit at 8-12x today and the tag adds nothing over an untagged hit. This one needs the extra
  assumption that the rework aimed at parity, which the note never says.
- The dummy test (Shielding Strike run vs Oath Strike run) tells the two apart: a ratio near 2.7
  = layered, near 1 = alternative.
- DPS / healer damage-to-threat: about 1x.

## Unknown (untested; leave blank in the chart)

- Exact per-power multipliers after the rework.
- Whether control powers generate threat beyond their damage.
- Whether incoming (self) healing generates threat identically to outgoing.
- Whether blocked damage generates threat.

## Measurement protocol

Tank + one DPS on a training dummy with a combat log (ACT on PC; the capture-card combat log parser
on console). No other threat sources: no Intimidating Presence, no taunts, no threat items. The tank
uses ONE power until the DPS pulls aggro. At the flip:

    multiplier = DPS damage so far / (1.1 x tank damage so far)

Run once with an untagged power (Shielding Strike) and once with a tagged one (Oath Strike); the
ratio of the two results is the Increased Threat tag's value. A third run with Intimidating Presence
on should read about 1.33x the first.

What’s Coming in Module 33.5

Straight from the official release article (September 1, 2026). The module revisits four existing zones as a 12-week event campaign.

Monoliths of Madness Event A 12-week event campaign revisiting Menzoberranzan, Northdark Reaches, Narbondellyn, and Dragonbone Vale. One zone is the mainstage each week (marked with a modified map icon) and everyone is downscaled while it is active. Start it by speaking to Master Arcanist Viorel Amakiir in Protector’s Enclave. If you have not finished the active zone’s own campaign yet, you progress in both at once.
Requirements to Join Level 20 minimum, plus zone access from Sergeant Knox in Protector’s Enclave:
• Menzoberranzan — ‘Whispers in the Dark’ + ‘A New Ally’
• Northdark Reaches — ‘The Call of the King’
• Narbondellyn — ‘Whispers in the Dark’ + ‘A Discreet Meeting’
• Dragonbone Vale — ‘Forging the Shield’
Global (server-wide) rewards also need at least 500 Shards of Madness earned.
Campaign & Shards of Madness Four phases of three weeks each. Daily and weekly challenges pay out Shards of Madness, which push you through campaign milestones. Milestone rewards include Warden’s Caches, Insignia Powder, Tradebars, Legendary Dragon Keys, and Mount & Companion Upgrade Tokens. The Staring Cat of Uldun-Dar companion comes from the ‘Can you hear him?’ phase. Rewards can be claimed on up to five characters per account; after that most convert to Warden’s Caches. A Monoliths of Madness Vendor also sells the Epic armor, shirts/pants and rings, Magnificent Illusion Essence, and more Warden’s Caches.
Nightmare Progression (Server-Wide) Every campaign phase also has a global progress bar filled by all player contribution. Clear a phase and everyone who earned at least 500 Shards of Madness is mailed that phase’s reward: Phase 1 gives Burning Hope (artifact), Phase 2 the Umbral Widow mount, Phase 3 the Brimming Bountiful Bag, and Phase 4 both the Peeper Keeper and the Champion of the Emerald Vigil title. If the server does not finish a phase, nobody gets that phase’s reward.
Corrupted Altars Each zone gets two corrupted altars. The first is opened with three Lesser Abyssal Lures, dropped by most enemies in the active zone. Kill what it summons and you can get a Greater Abyssal Lure — three of those open the second altar. Purifying altars is the single biggest source of Shards of Madness, and doing it in a group is the fastest way to move the server-wide bar.
World Boss: Maxare’xek the Mad A roaming world boss that appears every few hours. Killing it grants a one-hour buff that increases your damage inside the zone.
Corrupted Heroic Encounters The existing Heroic Encounters in each zone have been reworked with mechanics drawn from Reaper’s Challenge — burning ground, forced movement, a disease you have to cleanse, and meteor drops.
Two Combat Enchantment Slots You can now equip two Combat Enchantments. On the Enhancements tab the Utility slot moves down into the old Combat slot, freeing two Combat slots — one Strike and one Guard. Because Combat Enchantments give no Combined Rating but do add Item Level, their Item Level has been halved so two of them total the same Item Level as one did before. Your current enchantment is auto-slotted into one of the two. Combat Enchantments now increase Base Damage instead of Damage, so they no longer boost Outgoing Healing.
Item Stacks: 99 → 999 Stack size raised to 999 for Epic Collar Choice Packs, Genie’s Gifts, Potion of Healing Rank 10 (and Rank 10, +1), Black Pearls, and most Coalescent Motes, Overloads, Preservation Wards, and Stronghold Vouchers. Stacks do not auto-merge — drag one onto the other or use the sword button (bind statuses still do not mix).
Coffer of Celestial Insignias A new pickup on the Celestial Synergy tab of the Vault of Piety. Once per account per week for 11 Celestial Coins: a guaranteed 50 Insignia Powder plus either a Rare or an Epic Insignia — a steady passive Insignia Powder stream.

Staring Cat of Uldun-Dar — Legendary Companion From the Monoliths of Madness campaign, Phase 4 (Nov 3 – Nov 24).
Enhancement: Unflinching Will — Chance on hit to increase your and your companion’s Deflect and Deflect Severity for 15 seconds. Maximum 4.5%.
Defense Power: Grim Omen — Grants Movement Speed, Mount Speed, and Combined Rating.
Umbral Widow — Epic Mount From the Nightmare Progression.
Equip Power: Shadow Sight — Grants Movement Speed, Awareness, and Combined Rating.
Combat Power: Tunnel Vission — A strong single-target attack. (Spelled that way in the official article.)
Burning Hope — Epic Artifact From the Nightmare Progression.
Grants Power, Critical Severity, Outgoing Healing, and Combined Rating.
Use: Healing Flame — Charred petals leave a burning trail that invigorates instead of damaging you. Restores stamina and grants Outgoing Healing and Power.
Warden’s Cache The campaign’s repeatable reward box, and what most milestone rewards convert to after your fifth character. Chance at Teal Diamonds, Violet Diamonds, Preservation Wards, Insignia Powder, Glyphs of Potency, Common Enchantments, or Enhanced Motes.
Brimming Bountiful Bag (90 slots) A 90-slot bag, from Phase 3 of the Nightmare Progression.
Peeper Keeper A Backpack, from Phase 4 of the Nightmare Progression. Recovered from the monoliths; it keeps an eye on your loot.

Battle Pass & Lockbox

Both launch alongside the module on September 1, 2026. Values below come from the official articles and are not in-game verified.

Of Minds and Madness Battle Pass September 1, 2026 (after maintenance) → October 8, 2026, 7:30 AM PT for Phase 1. Two phases with nine milestones, plus a Premium Reward Track that unlocks a second reward at every milestone.
Laughing Void — Artifact (Battle Pass Phase 1) Grants Critical Strike, Critical Severity, Combat Advantage, Stamina Regeneration, and Combined Rating.
Use: Corrupting Form — For 10 seconds, tendrils absorb your Hit Points and grant Critical Strike and Outgoing Damage.
Set: while also wearing the Voidbound Necklace and Voidbound Belt, Corrupting Form drains more Hit Points and additionally grants Combat Advantage, Movement Speed, and Accuracy.
Voidbound Necklace & Voidbound Belt — Mythic Voidbound Necklace — Strength, Dexterity, Intelligence, Combat Advantage, Critical Severity, Forte, Combined Rating.
Voidbound Belt — Strength, Dexterity, Intelligence, Accuracy, Power, Critical Severity, Combined Rating.
Both feed the Laughing Void set effect above.
Battle Pass Phase 2 Free track: Pika the Tressym (shoulder buddy), a Coalescent Mote, and assorted rewards. Premium track: a Legendary Enchantment Choice Pack offering Swift Synergy, Radiant Sanctuary, or Rime Temper.
Wild Adventures Lockbox September 1, 2026 → November 17, 2026. Headline drops are the Star Angler mount and Encore the Virtuoso companion.
Star Angler — Mythic Mount (Lockbox) Equip Power: Uncanny Precision — Grants 2,700 Combined Rating, 2,250 Combat Advantage, and 2,250 Accuracy.
Combat Power: Devouring Depths — Summons the Star Angler at a target location; it pulls enemies into a whirlpool and erupts for a total magnitude of 750 over 4 seconds. Reduces the target’s outgoing damage and Combat Advantage for 10 seconds, and increases their incoming damage for 10 seconds.
Encore the Virtuoso — Mythic Companion (Lockbox) A kenku minstrel.
Mending Melody — heals up to 5 allies and buffs their Outgoing Healing, Power, and Critical Severity.
Strings on Fire — damages an enemy and increases their Incoming Damage.
Tune of Order — damages and pulls in nearby enemies, reducing their Outgoing Damage and Movement Speed.
Enhancement — additional Combined Rating.
Defense Power: Bard’s Finesse — Critical Severity, Combined Rating, Outgoing Healing Bonus, and Power.

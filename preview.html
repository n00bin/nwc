What’s Coming in Module 34

Straight from the official preview article (October 19, 2026). A full module this time: a new Icewind Dale adventure zone, a trial, a skirmish, a brand-new difficulty, and upgradeable Artifact Armor.

Adventure Zone: The Reghed Crevasse In the far northeastern corner of Icewind Dale, the Cult of the Elder Elemental Eye is spreading madness through the North and digging up ancient Netherese city ruins under the ice. Start by speaking with Sergeant Knox. Old Gravity Devices carry you up to high or remote spots, and a new series of Heroic Encounters stops the cult from getting the Netherese knowledge. Open for testing from October 19.
Zone Mechanic: Madness of the Sleeping God Activities in the zone fill a meter that leads to the Madness Rift. There you choose one of up to three Afflictions: each gives a powerful benefit and a dangerous drawback. Once Madness takes hold, hidden Rift Fractures, Fractured Echoes, and Haunting Horrors appear. With at least three players you can summon and fight Valkreth the Hollow. This is also where Chaotic Flux comes from (see Artifact Armor). From October 19.
Trial: The Devourer’s Pit A scorching troll arena. Face the troll champion Grathmok the Devourer and his savage wrestling moves, and watch your step around his shockwaves or you burn. Four difficulties: Normal, Advanced, Master, and the new Ascendant Mode. Normal to Master open for testing October 23–26.
New Difficulty: Ascendant Mode An extremely hard version of select Master content: more enemy Hit Points and damage, plus modifiers that change over four rotating weeks, so a class or plan that works one week may not work the next. One dungeon (in a future module) and one trial (The Devourer’s Pit) at a time. Arc says it is balanced heavily against players and most groups are not expected to clear it. Fully optional: Master stays the main source of endgame rewards. Testing October 30 – November 2.
Ascendant Rewards Clearing the weekly challenge gives a decaying currency for the new Bahamut’s Store in Protector’s Enclave, which sells Mythic and Celestial versions of the module’s chase gear. The currency comes once per week; you can keep playing, but no more is granted. Clear all the different weeks for an exclusive alternate version of Master Mode’s achievement vanity reward, once per account. It drops as a container: sell it as it is, or open it to unlock it for all your characters (it is then untradeable).
Skirmish: The Defense of the Mythallar A Harbinger has sealed a mythallar’s dome in a shell to channel enough power to snuff out the North. Break through the tower wall, fight through Netherese fortifications, disarm traps while Elemental Evil cultists close in, and stop the ritual. Drops three Amethyst neck items (see the rewards table). From October 19.
Artifact Armor This module’s chase gear (Mod 32 was artifact weapons, Mod 33 rings, shirts and pants). Buy the armor at Normal rarity from the Trial Store with Unquiet Whispers, the new unlimited zone currency. Upgrading skips Uncommon, Rare and Epic and goes straight to Legendary → Mythic → Celestial through normal Refining, with currency from The Devourer’s Pit. Chaotic Flux unlocks a new stat on a piece or tries to raise one; a failed try keeps your best value so far. Each piece has its own short list of stats it can get. From October 26.
Queue Rewards & Seals Normal Queues now also drop Mounts, Companions, Enchantments, Artifacts, Neck and Waist items, and Companion Equipment. Seals of the Dragon buy anything from that list you missed, or Epic gear, including this module’s. Seals of the Spider God are retired: every source (Advanced, Master and Random Trial queues) now gives Seals of the Dragon, and old Spider God seals trade in at 3:1. Random Advanced Dungeons, past and future, now need 70,000 Item Level. New Seals of the Ancient Dragon buy Legendary gear up to Mod 32: The Soul Collector. From October 19.
Experimental Graphics New options on the Advanced tab of the settings: Volumetric Lighting (light in fog), Volumetric Cloud and Shadow (3D fog and clouds that scatter light), Parallax Occlusion Mapping (extra depth on some objects; nothing in the game uses it yet), Foot to Ground IK (feet sit on stairs and slopes instead of floating, with a standing-still-only option), and Screen Space Reflections. They roll in gradually, starting with the new zone. The preview is PC-only, and the article doesn’t say whether these come to PS5.

The article says “travel to the Aberrant Crevasse” once and “Reghed Crevasse” everywhere else. Needs an in-game check.

Arcanist (DPS, magical)
Power+50%
Combat Advantage+25%
Critical Avoidance+25%
Arcane Presence — Arcane Mastery increases the damage of your Cold, Fire, and Lightning based attacks by 1% per stack.
Evocation — Increase the damage of your Area of Effect powers by 10%.
Chilling Presence — Increase the damage you deal by 0.5% for each stack of Chill on your target. This damage bonus is doubled on Frozen targets.
Orb of Imposition — Increase the duration of your control powers by 25%. Your control powers do 5% more damage against control immune targets.
Eye of the Storm — Using an Encounter or Daily power grants you Eye of the Storm for 5 seconds. While active, Eye of the Storm grants you 10% Critical Strike. This effect can only occur once every 10 seconds.
Storm Spell — Shock your target for 120 magnitude 20% of the time on critical attacks.
Storm Fury — When attacked by an enemy, they are shocked with a bolt from the storm doing 200 magnitude damage. This effect can only trigger on an enemy once every 3 seconds.
Arcane Power Field — When you activate a Daily power you gain Arcane Power Field for 8 seconds. Arcane Power Field doubles the damage bonus granted by stacks of Arcane Mastery and causes foes within 30 ft of you to take 60 magnitude damage every 2 seconds.
Thaumaturge (DPS, magical)
Power+50%
Combat Advantage+25%
Critical Avoidance+25%
Arcane Presence — Arcane Mastery increases the damage of your Cold, Fire, and Lightning based attacks by 1% per stack.

Swath of Destruction — Increases Smolder damage by 10%, and targets affected by Smolder take 3% more damage.
Combustive Action — When a target affected by Smolder dies, you gain 5% of your max Action Points. Cooldown 1 second.
Frost Wave — When you activate a Daily power you apply 6 stacks of Chill and Freeze foes within 30 ft of you.

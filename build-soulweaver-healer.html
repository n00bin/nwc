Where the mid-fight number comes from

Six Outgoing Healing bonuses in this build only switch on under a condition. They fall into three kinds, and only the first two show up in the town-versus-fight gap.

Combat-only

Zero standing in town. On once the fight starts.

  • Gladiator’s Restoration · Armor · +1% per 5s in combat, and +1% Crit Strike, up to 5 stacks+5%
  • Circular Healing · Ring · for 10s whenever you get healed in combat+8%
  • Unleashed · Weapons · once the Impending Doom set charges over the fight+5%
Strictly combat+18%

Party-gated

On the moment you group, fight or no fight.

  • Graceful Harmony · Shirt · +1.4% for each player in your team; teammates within 20’ get +1.2% Forteup to +7%
Party onlyup to +7%

Behaviour-gated

Already on in town if you’re healthy, so they don’t show in the town-versus-fight gap. These are the ones you control by how you play — and the ones you lose by dodge-rolling and sitting at half health.

  • Survivor’s Instinct · Head · while you’re above 90% health+9%
  • Rested Healing · Pants · while stamina is above 75%+9%
  • Deity’s Gift · Ring · Power, scaling with current hit points+8.5% Power
Healing you keep by staying healthy · plus the 8.5% Power+18%
The playstyle lesson.

This build pays you to stay healthy, stop dodge-rolling constantly, and stand where the heals land. A healer who sits at half health and rolls through every mechanic is a worse healer in this gear. The numbers say so.

Why Dexterity, not Charisma? Dexterity feeds Crit Severity, and the movement speed is a bonus. Charisma would give Forte and Recharge Speed; recharge does little for a healer, and this build already gets more Forte from gear than a few ability points would ever add.

The fine print

What the optimizer was told
Dungeon content, a full support party assumed, party-pack meta on, companion bolster 120, mount bolster 125, Wood Elf with +2 Wisdom. Nothing locked, nothing pinned — this is its free pick. If you solo-queue randoms, load the build, switch “assume support party” off, and re-run; the optimal picks shift a little.
Where the numbers on this page come from
The town and mid-fight figures are the character’s live Stats screen, captured solo in Protector’s Enclave and then grouped mid-fight in Master Shrieking Halls on the same day. The +25 is the ceiling of the four combat- and party-gated bonuses; the screen shows +17 and a cap, because the cap hides the rest.
Every item was checked
All 70 items, every power, feat and boon were verified against the Compendium’s data pack before publication, and effect text on this page follows that data. Tooltips in game are the final word — if one disagrees, use the Reports page.
The one thing that was changed after the optimizer
The summoned companion, and only when a party member already has the same one out. Everything else is as the optimizer built it.

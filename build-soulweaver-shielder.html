One feat makes it a shielder

No piece of gear on this character mentions shields. The feat is the whole conversion, and it’s the last one on the list.

Feypact — the healer build

Vampiric Embrace, Revitalize, Soulstorm and your lifespark’s heal all leave a heal over time. You top people up after they get hit.

  • Best when the damage is constant and ticking.
  • The heal-over-time build →

Hellpact — this build

Those same four leave an Infernal Barrier instead, sized from the heal, and Infernal Sanction’s barrier gets stronger. You soak the damage before it reaches the health bar.

  • Best when survivability is the problem in the dungeon.
  • Even full-health allies get the shield, so you can put it up before the damage comes.
The catch: resource.

A heal-over-time keeps working for seconds after you cast. A shield doesn’t — once it’s gone, you cast again. A shielder casts more often, and every heal and every shield costs Soulweave. That’s why the regen moved to the helm.

The fine print

What I wear on camera
The video shows a few pieces that differ from the linked build, and either version works. Feet: on camera I wear the Prismatic Luminstep Greaves with Ruthless Resources (regen, up to 5 stacks) so the helm and boots stack; the link has the Luminstep Boots. Summoned companion: Portobello DaVinci on camera, Tutor in the link. Overload: a Giant Ward on camera, Demon Ward in the link. Boons: my live character has 129 points spent; the link shows 99.
One slip in the video
In the powers section I say the shields on Vampiric Embrace and Revitalize come “because of Feypact”. I meant Hellpact.
What the optimizer was told
A healer-role run with Hellpact slotted. Dungeon content, no support party assumed, party-pack meta on, companion bolster 120, mount bolster 125, Wood Elf with +2 Wisdom. Nothing locked, nothing pinned.
Where the numbers on this page come from
The 65% and the 1,000-magnitude Infernal Sanction shield are read off the in-game tooltips with Hellpact slotted. The town figures are my Stats screen unbuffed in the training room; the fight figures are the same character fully buffed in combat in Master Shrieking Halls. Mount power values are my live tooltips.
Every item was checked
Every item, power, feat and boon in the linked build was verified against the Compendium’s data pack before publication. Tooltips in game are the final word — if one disagrees, use the Reports page.
When to run it
This is a second loadout, not a replacement for the healer build. You can’t change loadouts once a fight starts, so pick before the pull: when survivability is the problem in the dungeon, switch to this one.

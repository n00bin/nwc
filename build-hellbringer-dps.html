The Curse loop

Seven buttons, one idea. Put the Curse on, spend it for extra value, and cash every hit in as Soul Sparks.

1 · Put it on

  • All-Consuming Curse — your at-wills (Dark Helix, Hellish Rebuke) apply Curse.
  • Killing Flames applies it too, and both dailies — Tyrannical Curse and Soul Siphon — Curse what they hit.
  • Warlock’s Curse — +15% damage to Cursed targets.

2 · Spend it

  • Hadar’s Grasp on a Cursed target: +150 magnitude and a Soul Puppet. The Curse is used up — the next at-will puts it back.
  • Vampiric Embrace on a Cursed target: doubles the health you steal back. That one is your self-heal, not extra damage.

3 · Cash it in

  • Every power hit builds Soul Sparks; with Dark Prayers, every puppet hit adds one more.
  • Soul Scorch spends up to 18 at once. Double Scorch: +25 magnitude per spark spent.
  • Soul Spark Recovery: every 6 sparks spent take 1 second off your encounter cooldowns.
It gets stronger as the boss gets weaker.

Executioner’s Gift adds up to 30% damage as the target’s health drops, and Killing Flames hits harder the lower they are. Soul Desecration keeps the puppet out for good, at double damage, and re-summons it if it’s ever gone.

What Soul Spark Recovery does here

It takes 1 second off your encounter cooldowns for every 6 sparks you spend, so a full 18-spark Soul Scorch trims up to 3 seconds per Scorch. Fewer sparks, smaller cut.

The fine print

One change from the AI
The optimizer built every slot except the overload setup, which N00binHard changed for the dungeon he was running. Match your overloads to your content; Rage of Flames works anywhere.
What the optimizer was told
A DPS-role run in dungeon content with a full support party assumed (party-pack meta on, group equip stacks on), companion bolster 120, mount bolster 125, Wood Elf with +2 Intelligence. Nothing locked, nothing pinned.
Best in a full group
Portobello DaVinci’s party bonus needs a full party within 50 feet, and Providence stacks highest when the whole group runs it. Solo or in a group without that support, those two do less — the rest of the build is unchanged.
Celestial vs Mythic
Everything here is Celestial. In N00binHard’s experience the Mythic versions of the same pieces run just as well — same effects, smaller numbers.

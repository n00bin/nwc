Companion Bolster

Every companion you own adds to your Companion Bolster, and the amount depends on its rarity. Only your best 10 companions count, so the ceiling is 120% — exactly ten Celestial companions. Bolster multiplies what your summoned companion contributes to your item level, so raising it lifts every stat that item level feeds.

RarityBolster givenTen of them
Common0.5%5%
Uncommon1%10%
Rare2%20%
Epic3%30%
Legendary5%50%
Mythic10%100%
Celestial12%120% (cap)

100% is the baseline, not the maximum — the bar runs to 120%. You can read your own figure on the character sheet, next to the companion item level contribution. Mounts use a separate bolster with its own table and a 125% ceiling.
